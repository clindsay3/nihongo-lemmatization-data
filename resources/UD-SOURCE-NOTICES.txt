Universal Dependencies and Wikidata source notices

The Simplemma 2.0.0 source at revision
22a19bd457e332fd9b109ebf30ffdbb867d999ad documents an override layer mined from
Universal Dependencies training treebanks. Its downloader selects UD 2.18;
the miner pools all available training treebanks for each language. The
committed overrides are then merged into the packaged dictionaries.
https://github.com/adbar/simplemma/tree/22a19bd457e332fd9b109ebf30ffdbb867d999ad/training

Credits belong to the Universal Dependencies project and each source
treebank's authors and contributors. The corresponding project pages link
the treebanks and their individual credits and licenses:
https://universaldependencies.org/
https://universaldependencies.org/de/index.html
https://universaldependencies.org/fr/index.html
https://universaldependencies.org/es/index.html
https://universaldependencies.org/ru/index.html
https://universaldependencies.org/nl/index.html

Source history is not available for every entry. LICENSE-PROVENANCE.json
records the pinned generation scripts and committed override files. A matching
word/lemma pair does not establish exclusive origin. Training inputs must be
distinguished from datasets used only for evaluation; this notice does not
assert that every treebank listed by UD contributed a shipped mapping.

French ParTUT (UD 2.18)

The treebank was developed at the University of Turin. Contributors identified
by the source: Cristina Bosco and Manuela Sanguinetti.
https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/UD_French-ParTUT/tree/r2.18
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/UniversalDependencies/UD_French-ParTUT/r2.18/README.md
The dataset declares Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0.
Its original notice and full license text are included in
UD-FRENCH-PARTUT-LICENSE.txt. Trailing line whitespace is removed; wording is
unchanged.

Russian SynTagRus (UD 2.18)

The source treebank is developed by the Computational Linguistics Laboratory,
A.A. Kharkevich Institute of Information Transmission Problems, Russian Academy
of Sciences. UD data contributors identified by the source: Kira Droganova,
Olga Lyashevskaya and Daniel Zeman.
https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/UD_Russian-SynTagRus/tree/r2.18
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/UniversalDependencies/UD_Russian-SynTagRus/r2.18/README.md

Its original license notice is included verbatim in
UD-RUSSIAN-SYNTAGRUS-LICENSE.txt; the full CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 text is also included
in UD-FRENCH-PARTUT-LICENSE.txt.

Wikidata Lexeme fill

The pinned Simplemma builder allows a Wikidata fill layer for German, Spanish,
Russian and Dutch; French is explicitly excluded. Credits belong to Wikidata
Lexeme contributors. Wikidata's licensing policy releases structured Lexeme
data under CC0:
https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
The extracted fill files are not included in the upstream Git tree, so this
notice does not claim an exact contribution count for that layer.
